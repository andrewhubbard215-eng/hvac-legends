Support

Clock in, then open the six shop courses from the shop floor. Finish the lab in front of you before the next lab in that course unlocks. Opening a lab does not count. Finishing the work does.

Open the six shop courses

On the shop floor, tap Curriculum. The six courses are on that screen. Tap a lab button on the course to open it.

  • Bench — Ohms lab. Meter first. One right reading. Lock out before a live check.
  • Copper — Flare a line, then braze with nitrogen flowing through the joint.
  • Charge — Manifold school, recovery hookup, then a service call. Read superheat and subcooling before you add gas.
  • Circuit — Land a device, the Saturday callback, then the fault board. Do not jump a safety.
  • Install — Mini-split install, compressor change-out, then the furnace sequence. Do not skip the vacuum.
  • Card — Which 608 type, how empty, the cylinder, the three R’s, then the 608 drill. The drill is practice. It is not the official proctored exam.

Save for offline

While Wi-Fi works, open the Instructor Desk from the shop floor. On that desk, tap Save for offline. Do this before you leave coverage. The saved copy is what the phone uses when the network is down.

A lab is blank

Force-close HVAC Legends and open it again. Do not clear storage first. Clear storage deletes the locker on this device: callsign, locker photo, password, XP, lab progress, and material notes.
