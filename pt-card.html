Bubble is the liquid side. Dew is the vapor side. A pure refrigerant has one column.

R-22 · one pressure · A1Phased down. GWP 1810. Do not mix it with R-410A oil or fittings.
R-410A · bubble ≈ dew · A1GWP 2088. The two columns are the same number in the field. 118.8 psig at 40°F.
R-454B · two columns · A2LGWP 466. About 2°F of glide. Bubble for subcooling. Dew for superheat.
R-32 · one pressure · A2LGWP 675. Pure. 121 psig at 40°F, 206 at 70°F, 326 at 100°F.
