## Full description

Paste the block below. Limit is 4000 characters.

```
HVAC Legends is a vocational HVAC trainer. Clock in on your phone and work the shop floor. The labs are the lesson.

System sandbox. Run the refrigeration cycle and watch the system respond.

Manifold school. Hook the manifold. Read superheat and subcooling. Call the charge from the numbers, not from a guess.

Recovery hookup. Set the tank, the recovery machine, the hoses, and the core tool before you pull refrigerant.

Mini-split install. Flare, vacuum, weigh-in, and the line-set path. Do the steps in order.

Compressor change-out. Recover, lock out, braze in the new compressor, fit a new drier, evacuate, and weigh in.

Flaring lab and Brazing lab. Nut on the tube first. Make a clean flare. Flow nitrogen while you braze. A photo of the joint is not the same as a joint that holds.

Land lugs. Land the electrical lugs. Hot, neutral, and ground go on the screws they belong on.

Saturday callback. A no-cool. Meter first and isolate the open. This is a service ticket, not a weapon. Techs sometimes call that Saturday job a callback bomb. There is no explosive in this app.

EPA 608 tutor. Practice for Core, Type I, Type II, and Type III. This is not the official exam and it does not issue a certification card.

All-Star Exam. OSHA-style safety practice: falls, lockout, PPE, electrical, and heat. Open All-Star Exam and choose OSHA 30. It is practice, not an OSHA card.

Furnace SOO. The furnace sequence from the call for heat through inducer, pressure switch, ignitor, gas valve, flame signal, and blower.

iConnect lab. An iConnect-style fault board. Find the fault. Do not jump a safety. HVAC Legends is not an official iConnect product.

Curriculum. A 6-course shop path: Bench, Copper, Charge, Circuit, Install, and Card. Finish the lab and the course moves.

Professor HUB coaches while you work. Optional lines can be blunt shop talk. Turn the roast down in class. Practice answers on EPA 608 stay about the work.

Works offline after a save. No ads. Progress stays on the phone. You do not need an account to run the labs.

For apprentices and working techs. Not directed at children under 13.
```

## Data safety (same facts as DATA-SAFETY.md)

- Collected? Core labs, no. Callsign, optional locker photo, lab progress, and XP stay on the phone. Optional network calls (class chat, a class PIN, review notes) can leave the phone only if the user uses those features. For the Play question “does the app collect or share any required user data types?” answer Yes because of those optional calls.
- Shared? No. Not sold. Not used for ads. Not shared with data brokers.
- Encrypted in transit? The locker is not transmitted. Check Yes for the optional calls only when that host is HTTPS. If a class is reached over plain HTTP, check No.

- Permissions in the Android wrapper: INTERNET and VIBRATE only.
